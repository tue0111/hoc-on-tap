## Why promotion fails

After promoting KTCP and KTNH, the existing `owners` check in
`retrieval/partition.py` considers newly enabled course concepts whenever the
requested course has no detected concept. This makes KTCP a competing owner and
changes KTTC2 coverage despite unchanged evidence ranking and scores.

- `k2-s02`, `k2-s03`, `k2-c01`: sufficient → insufficient.
- `k2-i02`, `k2-i03`: unknown reasons/order change.
- LKD `lk-i02`: unknown reasons/order change.
- Chunk ranks and scores remain identical across all seven courses.

Evidence: `r1_promotion_diff.json` and `r1_enabled_non_targets.json`.
This violates A2's requirement that other-course output be byte-identical, even
though rank/score alone is identical. Do not hide the regression by ignoring
coverage, accepting only aggregate gate scores, or changing the frozen evals.
